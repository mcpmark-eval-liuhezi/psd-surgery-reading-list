# PSD Surgery Reading List — Pilonidal Sinus Disease

A curated, ranked reading list for Friday's journal club on the **surgical treatment of pilonidal sinus disease (PSD)**, focusing on **long-term recurrence** and **postoperative infection** across techniques.

## Research question

> **Which surgical technique gives the lowest long-term recurrence in pilonidal sinus disease?**

Papers were shortlisted from two PubMed searches (see *Methods*) if they reported **comparative outcomes across surgical techniques with meaningful follow-up**. The list is ranked so that the paper addressing the research question most directly sits at #1.

## Guidelines and web sources

### European Society of Coloproctology (ESCP) guidelines for the management of pilonidal disease

**Ojo D, Gallo G, Kleijnen J, et al.** *British Journal of Surgery* 2024;111(10):znae237. [PMID: 39397672](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39397672/)

**URL:** <https://doi.org/10.1093/bjs/znae237>

**Summary.** The ESCP guideline — the current European consensus reference to read alongside the shortlist — recommends off-midline closure as the preferred technique when excisional surgery is performed (moderate-certainty evidence) and explicitly advises that excision with primary midline closure should be avoided. For asymptomatic or minimal disease it favours a non-operative approach, while phenol, laser and endoscopic (EPSiT) treatments may be offered in selected patients, and acute pilonidal abscess should be drained via a lateral incision. Rather than declaring a single "best" operation, it offers a staged management algorithm (asymptomatic → minimal → extensive/recurrent disease) intended as a framework for local practice, with an update planned within three years.
